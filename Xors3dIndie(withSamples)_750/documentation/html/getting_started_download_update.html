Downloading and Updating
Xors3d Trial can be downloaded from http://xors3d.com/licesing (installer).
Xors3d Indie can be downloaded from http://updates.xors3d.com (zip or installer).
There are two places where you can get the updates of Xors3d.
  • The first one is http://hot.xors3d.com (Hot Area).
    This website contains a full changelog and the links to the latest revision (in the top menu and in the blue-orange banner at the very top of the page). Also you can subscribe to RSS feed to keep the track of new revisions.
    At the Hot Area we publish only the most necessary files: dlls, headers and documentation.
  • The second place is http://updates.xors3d.com (Closed Area).
    This website is available for customers only.
    At the Closed Are we publish only those files which can't be shared with public. That's why not every update is published there. If there isn't the latest revision, update from http://hot.xors3d.com
